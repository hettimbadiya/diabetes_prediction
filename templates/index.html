About DiabetesCare AI Platform

A full-stack clinical machine-learning system built to assist healthcare researchers and practitioners in identifying early diabetes biomarker patterns.

Machine Learning Pipeline

1
Dataset Ingestion & De-duplication

Loads diabetes_prediction_dataset.csv and filters duplicate rows to prevent overfitting.

2
Categorical Feature Encoding

Encodes gender and smoking_history using scikit-learn LabelEncoder.

3
StandardScaler Normalization

Scales feature vectors with zero mean and unit variance. The fitted scaler is reused for individual web inference.

4
Support Vector Classifier (SVC)

Trains an RBF kernel Support Vector Machine with hyperplanes maximizing clinical margin separation.

5
Multi-Model Benchmarking

Simultaneously evaluates KNN, Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, and Random Forests.

Clinical Input Features

  • gender: Biological sex (Female, Male, Other).
  • age: Chronological age in years (0 - 120).
  • hypertension: History of diagnosed hypertension (0 = No, 1 = Yes).
  • heart_disease: History of diagnosed heart disease (0 = No, 1 = Yes).
  • smoking_history: Smoking exposure (never, current, former, ever, not current, No Info).
  • bmi: Body Mass Index in kg/m² (Weight / Height²).
  • HbA1c_level: Glycated hemoglobin percentage (Normal: <5.7%, Prediabetes: 5.7–6.4%, Diabetes: ≥6.5%).
  • blood_glucose_level: Fasting or postprandial glucose in mg/dL (Normal: 70–139 mg/dL).
